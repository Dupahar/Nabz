# Nabz (नब्ज़): the pulse of every field conversation

**Kirana-counter intelligence for Indian FMCG brands, built on Gnani AI's Prisma and Timbre.**

**Problem.**
- 89% of India's offline FMCG sales go through traditional trade (NIQ, Q1 2026).
- Why an outlet switched to a competitor, which scheme confused it, and what isn't selling are said
  out loud to a distributor salesman, in Hinglish, at the counter. The brand never hears it.
- Retail audits show what sold, not why. SFA apps log orders, not conversations.
- Meanwhile quick commerce (a projected $65–70B by 2030, Bain) and competitor credit are taking the counter.

**Product.**
- The rep records a visit after the owner consents (mobile web, no install).
- Gnani Prisma transcribes the noisy, code-mixed speech, separates the speakers, and uses the brand's vocabulary.
- Personal data is redacted.
- An FMCG lens scores the visit and extracts **market signals, each backed by a quote that code checks against the transcript**.
- Gnani Timbre speaks a 20-second coaching debrief in the rep's language.
- Leaders see competitor share of voice, scheme confusion, demand and price sensitivity by region.
- CRM changes need human approval. A tamper-evident audit log records everything.

**Customer.**
- Who: FMCG and food brands with ₹500–5,000 Cr turnover, 200–2,000 field reps and distributor salesmen, and over 70% general trade.
- Buyer: Head of Sales, with the CMO.
- First use case: trade-scheme and competitor intelligence, plus coaching.

**Business model.**
- Pulse add-on to the existing SFA: ₹600–800 per salesman per month.
- Intelligence: ₹1,500 per rep per month.
- Brand platform fee: ₹2–5 lakh a year.
- Accounts: land at about ₹14 lakh a year, expand to about ₹37 lakh.
- About 54% gross margin with Haiku routing and sampled recording.
- **Pricing is not yet validated.** The first milestone is 3 LOIs.

**Market (FMCG only).**
- TAM ₹144–672 Cr a year (4–8 lakh field users × an SFA-anchored price).
- SAM ~₹231 Cr.
- Funnel-based plan: year 1 is 2 brands (~₹28 lakh ARR); year 3 is ~12 brands (~₹2.5–3 Cr ARR).

**Why now.**
1. Code-mixed Indic speech recognition is usable and cheap (Prisma v3.0, Oct 2026; ~₹20–27 per hour).
2. LLM analysis costs under a rupee per visit.
3. Kiranas are under quick-commerce pressure, so brands need counter-level signals faster.
4. In-person conversation intelligence is proven in the US (Rilla, Siro, Salesforce 2026).

**Competition.**
- Convin has an in-person recorder.
- VoClyp is an early entrant.
- Bizom and FieldAssist own the rep's app, so they're a partner or a threat.
- Gnani is a partner and channel.
- **No moat today.** What could build up: a consented Indic field-conversation dataset, FMCG lens IP, and SFA distribution.

**Proof so far.**
- A working end-to-end product: recorder, Prisma, verified analysis, Timbre debrief, dashboard.
- 11 demo visits processed with 100% of quotes verified.
- An open Prisma vs Whisper noise benchmark using independent (ElevenLabs) voices.
